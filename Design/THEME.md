# Field Frames theme

Mira borrows the identity of the Field Frames photo site: a quiet gallery on a dark ink ground, warm paper text, one accent (lichen), Cormorant Garamond display type, IBM Plex Mono for small uppercase tracked labels, hairlines, grain, a character-cell grid, square corners, no glass.

Source: `Field-Frames/styles.css` (tokens at lines 25-45, cell grid 57-91, theme overrides 93-125, grain tiles 1627-1628) and the Theme / Grain sections of its `CLAUDE.md`. Code: `Packages/MiraUI/Sources/MiraUI/Theme/`.

## Colour tokens

Dark is the identity, light is the CSS `light-dark()` light branch. Colours are dynamic `NSColor`s (appearance provider) wrapped as SwiftUI `Color`, so they follow the system appearance. The raw values are in `FieldFramesTheme.tokens` (`FFToken`).

| CSS token | Dark | Light | Swift colour / token |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--ink` | `#15140f` | `#e9e5d9` | `FieldFramesTheme.ink` / `inkToken` |
| `--ink-soft` | `#1d1b15` | `#ded9cc` | `inkSoft` |
| `--paper` | `#e9e5d9` | `#15140f` | `paper` |
| `--paper-soft` | `#d6d1c4` | `#2a2820` | `paperSoft` |
| `--paper-mid` | `#c9c4b6` | `#4a473e` | `paperMid` |
| `--paper-dim` | `#9c988b` | `#5c584d` | `paperDim` |
| `--lichen` | `#93a382` | `#54644a` | `lichen` |
| `--hair` | paper @ 12% | paper @ 12% | `hair` (`#e9e5d9`/`#15140f` at 0.12) |
| `--backdrop` | `rgba(12,11,8,.96)` | `rgba(240,237,229,.96)` | `backdrop` |
| `--on-photo`, `-soft`, `-mid`, `-dim`, `-accent` | `#e9e5d9 #d6d1c4 #c9c4b6 #9c988b #93a382` | same | `onPhoto`, `onPhotoSoft`, `onPhotoMid`, `onPhotoDim`, `onPhotoAccent` |
| `--on-photo-veil` | `rgba(12,11,8,.58)` | same | `onPhotoVeil` |

`onPhoto*` do not flip: they are for type that sits on a photograph.

## Type roles

| Role | CSS | Swift |
|---|---|---|
| Display | `--display` Cormorant Garamond | `FieldFramesTheme.display(_:weight:)`, `displayItalic`, `.ffDisplay(_:)` |
| Label / readout | `--mono` IBM Plex Mono, 0.462rem, uppercase, 0.14em | `FieldFramesTheme.mono(_:weight:)`, `.ffLabel(size: 10, color:)` (uppercase, 0.1em tracking, paper-dim) |
| Body / UI text | mono on the site | system font (deliberate: description copy and license text stay readable) |

Fonts are bundled (OFL, see `Resources/Fonts/`): Cormorant Garamond roman and italic variable fonts (weight axis 300-700, applied per call through the `wght` variation) and IBM Plex Mono Regular / Medium / SemiBold. `FieldFramesTheme.registerFonts()` registers them process-wide with `CTFontManagerRegisterFontURLs`; it is idempotent and called from `MiraApp.init` and the extension's `loadView`.

## Cell grid

`--cell-w` / `--cell-h` map to `FieldFramesTheme.cellW = 8`, `cellH = 16`; `cells(n)` is n cell widths, `rows(n)` n cell heights. Gutters are 2 cells (16 pt), control heights 1-2 rows, the film card is 14 cells wide with a 5-row thumbnail, the inspector is 36 cells.

## Grain

`FFGrain` generates a 256 px seeded, tileable noise tile once (SplitMix64, two-sample average, light wrapped blur, alpha = noise^2.4 like the site's feFuncA gamma) and caches it as white specks (dark) and black specks (light). `FFGrainView` / `.ffGrain(opacity:)` tile it at one tile pixel per device pixel, at 60% opacity in light (the site goes 0.16 to 0.10), `allowsHitTesting(false)`. Grain is only used in backgrounds (window ground 0.07, panels 0.07), never over the photograph.

## Library UI and toolbar

The Photos library browser (`App/Sources/PhotoLibrary/`) reuses the components above: the sidebar and grid sit on `FFGroundBackground` with `FFHairline` dividers, section and count labels use `.ffLabel`, and RAW and EDITED badges are small mono labels on the thumbnail. The window toolbar has no Liquid Glass capsule and no buttons: just the "Mira" wordmark in Cormorant on the ink ground with a hairline under it. Commands live in the menu bar. `.ffToolbarIcon` stays in the kit for other uses. The mono footer under the editor is hidden unless Settings > "Show edit-source footer" is on (`mira.showEditSourceFooter`).  About (Mira > About Mira) and Settings (⌘,) are Field Frames windows (`.fieldFramesSecondaryChrome()`): About shows the Cormorant wordmark, version, "Software by FieldFrames", the film list and the credits; Settings has a Cormorant heading, a mono GENERAL section label and `FFToggle`s on a panel. "Software by FieldFrames" is also an `FFByline` under the headline in empty states. The app and extension icons (ink ground, lichen Cormorant "M", paper hairline frame) are generated by `scripts/make-icons.swift`.

### Editor sub-toolbar and tabs

While a photo is open a strip (`EditorSubToolbar`) sits directly under the title bar across the full editor width: ink-soft with grain, 2.5 rows (40 pt) tall, a hairline along its bottom edge. Left: `← LIBRARY` (mono, paperMid; app only), a short vertical hairline, then the tabs FILMS · DEVELOP · CROP (mono uppercase; active tab paper, medium weight, with a 1 px lichen underline sitting on the bottom hairline; inactive paperMid). Trailing: a small mono status (paperMid for UNSAVED, lichen otherwise) and SAVE as an `.ffProminent` button (reads SAVED and is inert after a save; app only). The Photos extension shows the tabs only.

Tabs choose the surroundings of the canvas, which stays: FILMS shows the film strip under the canvas and the film header with STRENGTH in the right panel; DEVELOP shows the histogram, tone and color sliders, curves, color mixer, TEXTURE and FRAME with RESET at the panel foot (no strip); CROP shows the crop controls bar under the canvas and a crop readout panel (entering the tab begins the tool, leaving commits). The right panel is 300 to 340 pt wide and scrolls. F hides the strip, panel and film strip and draws the photo on `inkAlways` (the dark ink in both appearances); zoom readouts and the hover chip use the `onPhoto` veil chip at the canvas corners.

## What deliberately differs from the site

- The app follows the system appearance; the site defaults to dark and ignores the OS.
- Grain is a procedural tile, not the SVG `feTurbulence` data URI, and is drawn with an image paint rather than `body::after`.
- The cell is fixed at 8 x 16 pt; the site derives it from the viewport (`--vu`).
- Hairlines are 1 pt, not 1 CSS px of a scaled viewport.
- Body copy is system type; the site sets everything in mono or Cormorant.
- Cormorant is a variable font here; the site ships three static weights.
- No theme switch and no block cursor.
- Headless snapshots (`build/snapshots`) flatten scroll views (`FFScrollView` honours an internal `ffInlineScroll` environment flag) because `ImageRenderer` cannot draw AppKit-backed scroll views.
